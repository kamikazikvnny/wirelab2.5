Quick Instructions

  • Drag junction boxes to move them around the work area.
  • Click a junction box to select it.
  • Drag wiring devices into a junction box.
  • Drag 14/2-wire or 14/3-wire cables to knockout openings.
  • Drag the 3-prong appliance power cord to a knockout opening for its three-wire end.
  • Connect the 3-prong plug to the dedicated wall outlet at the edge of the work area.
  • The power cord provides hot, neutral, and ground through its three-wire end.
  • Click compatible terminals and conductors to make wiring connections.
  • Select a device and press Delete or Backspace to remove it.
  • Install a bulb into a light fixture when available.
